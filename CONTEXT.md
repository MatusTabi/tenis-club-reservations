# Tennis club reservations

## Language

**Court**: A tennis playing area that has one surface.

**Surface**: A court playing surface with a price per minute.

**Customer**: A person associated with a phone number and name who can hold reservations. Different customers may share a name.

**Reservation**: A customer's booking of one court for a time interval and game type, with a recorded total price.

**Game type**: Whether a reservation is for singles or doubles; doubles has a 1.5 price multiplier.

**Active reservation**: A reservation that has not been deleted, whether its interval is past, current, or future.

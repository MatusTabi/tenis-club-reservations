# BET-002: Court reservations

## Desired outcome

Customers can reserve a court and manage reservations through the API, with reliable availability and clear pricing.

## Problem

The club cannot yet record and manage court bookings, associate them with customers, or prevent conflicting bookings.

## Value

Customers and the club can make and manage reservations without double-booking courts and can see the price when booking.

## Scope

- In scope: Reservation creation, individual retrieval, full and partial updates, soft deletion, customer creation and reuse, pricing, listings of all reservations and by court number and phone number. Protect court deletion when bookings are ongoing or upcoming.
- Out of scope: Separate customer-management endpoints.
- Constraints: Each reservation belongs to one court and one customer; follow the existing court, surface, customer, and reservation model. Surface prices are in CZK per minute.

## Acceptance criteria

- Create a reservation for an active court with a customer name and phone number, singles or doubles game type, and explicit-offset start and end timestamps on whole-minute boundaries. The start is in the future and the duration is from 1 through 180 minutes.
- Calculate and return the recorded price as minutes times the court surface's price per minute, multiplied by 1.5 for doubles, rounded half up to two decimal places. Later rate changes do not change recorded prices; updates changing court, interval, or game type recalculate them.
- Match customers by phone number after trimming surrounding whitespace. Trim names and require an exact match for an existing phone number; reject mismatched names. Create a missing customer atomically with the reservation, and prevent duplicate customers under concurrent requests.
- Reject overlapping active reservations for the same court, including under concurrent requests; adjacent intervals and bookings on different courts are allowed.
- Retrieve active reservations individually, replace them with PUT, partially update them with PATCH, and soft-delete them. Reject updates or deletions once a reservation has started. Updates enforce creation's validation and availability rules. PATCH retains omitted fields, rejects null for required fields, and requires phone and name together when changing either.
- List all active reservations, or those for a phone number, with an option in either case to include only reservations starting in the future. Also list active reservations for an active court number. Sort all lists by creation time descending and ID descending. A nonexistent active court returns 404; a phone number with no matching reservations returns an empty list.
- Reservations follow their court when its number changes. Reusing a deleted court's number does not mix its historical reservations with the new court's. Reject deletion of a court with an ongoing or future active reservation.
- Cover business rules, persistence and API integration, concurrency, and critical end-to-end workflows with automated tests.

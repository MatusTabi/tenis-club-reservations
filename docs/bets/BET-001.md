# BET-001: Court and surface management

## Desired outcome

Manage tennis courts and their assigned surfaces through a REST API.

## Problem

The system needs operations to maintain courts and the surfaces available to them.

## Value

Court managers can maintain accurate court and surface information for the reservation system.

## Scope

- In scope: REST operations to create, list, retrieve, update, and delete courts and surfaces; assign a surface to each court.
- Out of scope: A user interface, authentication and authorization, and reservation management.
- Constraints: Follow the court and surface relationships in the class diagram; deletion is soft deletion. Surface prices are in CZK per minute.

## Acceptance criteria

- Courts and surfaces can be created, listed, retrieved by ID, fully updated with PUT, partially updated with PATCH, and soft-deleted.
- Each court has a positive whole-number court number and exactly one surface; a surface can be assigned to multiple courts.
- Active court numbers and trimmed, case-insensitive active surface names are unique. Names and numbers can be reused after deletion.
- Surface names are nonblank and at most 50 characters after trimming. Prices have two decimal places and range from 0.00 to 1000.00 CZK per minute inclusive.
- Deleting a surface assigned to an active court is rejected. Deleted records are excluded from normal lists and lookups; missing and deleted IDs return 404.
- Lists are unfiltered and unpaginated, ordered by court number or surface name. Court responses include surface ID, name, and price.
- PATCH leaves omitted fields unchanged and rejects explicit null for required fields, unknown fields, and empty updates.
